TRANSPARENT WORKFLOW

7-STEP EFFICIENT EXPORT PROCESS

We manage every phase of the export pipeline to ensure your shipment arrives on time with full regulatory compliance.

STEP 01

Requirement Discussion

Initial consultation regarding buyer product specs, quality parameters, MOQ, and destination port requirements.

STEP 02

Product & Supplier Sourcing

Selecting accredited processors, verifying lot availability, and finalizing preliminary samples.

STEP 03

Quotation & Commercial Terms

Providing competitive FOB/CIF quotation, payment terms (LC/TT), and schedule commitment.

STEP 04

Quality & Specification Confirmation

Finalizing lab testing parameters, packaging design, inner lining specs, and marking standards.

STEP 05

Export Documentation

Preparing Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, and Lab analysis reports.

STEP 06

Logistics & Container Loading

Stuffing containers under supervision at port/warehouse and initiating customs clearance coordination.

STEP 07

Shipment & Delivery Tracking

Vessel departure, issuing original Bill of Lading, tracking support, and post-delivery assistance.

QUALITY STANDARDS

QUALITY IS OUR COMMITMENT

We enforce structured supplier selection and quality coordination throughout the sourcing lifecycle.

Supplier Evaluation

Partnering exclusively with verified processors equipped with modern sorting, grading, and hygenic packaging facilities.

Specification Compliance

Ensuring products meet strict destination parameters for moisture, purity, mesh size, and volatile oil content.

Inspection Coordination

Facilitating third-party inspection agencies (such as SGS or Geo-Chem) based on buyer contract terms.

Packaging Integrity

Utilizing food-grade PP, Vacuum, Jute, or Multi-wall craft packaging designed for sea transit durability.

Documentation Protocol

Thorough verification of Phytosanitary certificates, Fumigation docs, and Health certificates.

Port Loading Oversight

Supervising container stuffing to guarantee cargo safety, weight accuracy, and moisture protection.

HELP & INFORMATION

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What products does INORA GLOBAL EXIM export?
We specialize in Indian agricultural and food products including Rice (Basmati, Ponni, Non-Basmati), Spices (Black Pepper, Turmeric, Cumin, Chili), Value-Added Botanical Powders (Moringa, Curry Leaf, Garlic), Fresh Fruits & Produce, Peanuts, and Dehydrated Vegetables.
Do you accept bulk FCL container orders?
Yes, bulk FCL (Full Container Load) shipments are our core focus. Minimum order quantities typically start at 1 x 20ft or 40ft container depending on product type.
Can you source products that are not listed in your catalogue?
Absolutely. As a dedicated Global Sourcing Partner, we leverage our extensive network of processors across India to locate and export custom products based on your specific requirements.
Do you provide customized private label packaging?
Yes, private labeling and buyer branding options (including pouch printing, customized cartons, and barcoding) are available subject to order quantity and supplier capability.
How can I request a formal price quotation?
You can fill out our website "Request a Quote" form, email us directly at exports@inoraglobal.com, or WhatsApp us at +91 72008 19993 with your product grade, volume, destination port, and packaging preference.
