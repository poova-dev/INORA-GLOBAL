END-TO-END EXPORT PROCESS

From specification alignment to vessel dispatch, we coordinate every document and freight milestone.

STEP 01

Requirement Discussion

Aligning buyer product grade, volume, packaging specifications, and port of destination.

STEP 02

Product & Supplier Sourcing

Selecting verified processors, inspecting raw batches, and confirming production timelines.

STEP 03

Quotation & Commercial Contract

Issuing transparent FOB/CIF pricing, LC or TT terms, and executing formal purchase agreement.

STEP 04

Quality & Specification Confirmation

Finalizing lab testing, packaging materials, container lining, and marking instructions.

STEP 05

Export Documentation

Preparing Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, and Fumigation docs.

STEP 06

Logistics & Container Stuffing

Supervising container loading at port/warehouse and initiating customs clearance coordination.

STEP 07

Delivery & Tracking Support

Vessel departure, issuing original Bill of Lading, transit tracking, and post-delivery follow-up.
