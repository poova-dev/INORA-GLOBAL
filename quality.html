QUALITY PROTOCOLS

QUALITY IS OUR COMMITMENT

Rigorous supplier evaluation, parameter verification, and pre-shipment coordination for international trade transparency.

Supplier Evaluation

Auditing manufacturer processing facilities, hygiene standards, and machinery setup.

Product Specifications

Verifying lot samples for moisture, grain length, mesh fineness, and purity percentages.

Supervising container stuffing to ensure weight accuracy, seal integrity, and cargo protection.
